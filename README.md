# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative approach to deliver cross-functional product features, services, and integrations. These documents describe the project lifecycle, the people involved, and the practices that help teams stay aligned, manage risk, deliver quality, and learn from each release.

## Project lifecycle

1. **Initiation** — Validate the business need and measurable outcome, identify stakeholders, agree on success criteria and an initial timeline, and decide whether to proceed to planning.
2. **Planning** — Turn the approved initiative into an actionable plan: prioritize and estimate the backlog, define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, map milestones and dependencies, and identify risks and responsibilities.
3. **Execution and tracking** — Deliver work in small increments and track it on the project board. Use pull requests with issue links and acceptance criteria, run automated checks, review and test changes, and share regular progress, risks, and blockers.
4. **Release and deployment** — Confirm acceptance criteria and CI/security checks are complete, prepare release notes and a rollback or mitigation plan, deploy and verify the release, and communicate it to stakeholders and support.
5. **Close and improve** — After a sprint, release, or milestone, review what went well and what could improve. Assign owners and due dates to a small number of action items and follow up on their impact.

Across the lifecycle, teams use clear ownership, customer-focused outcomes, regular stakeholder communication, and evidence from success metrics and operational signals to guide decisions. For risks and blockers, the documented escalation path runs from the team to the Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor as appropriate.
