# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation describes how OctoAcme initiates, plans, delivers, releases, and learns from cross-functional projects. It is intended to give team members a shared, accessible guide to project workflows, responsibilities, and working practices.

## Project management processes at a glance

OctoAcme begins with **initiation**: validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, and decide whether the work is ready for planning. During **planning**, the team turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified risks and dependencies. A named Project Manager coordinates delivery, while the Product Manager owns outcomes and prioritization; developers, QA, and stakeholders contribute their respective expertise.

During **execution**, the team tracks work on a project board, delivers in small increments, and uses pull requests for review. Automated tests, linting, security scans, and appropriate manual or end-to-end testing help protect quality. Regular standups and delivery or stakeholder updates make progress, blockers, risks, and decisions visible; issues that cannot be resolved at team level are escalated through the defined path.

For **release**, the team confirms acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and a rollback or mitigation plan. Deployments are verified and communicated to stakeholders, with incident response and rollback used when needed. After a sprint, release, milestone, or incident, a blameless retrospective captures learnings and a small set of owned, trackable improvement actions.
